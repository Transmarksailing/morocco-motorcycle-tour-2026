🏨 Morocco Motorcycle Tour 2026 — Hotel & Ferry Booking Sheet

Trip: Moraira → Morocco → Moraira · 3 riders, 3 bikes (2× BMW R1250GS + 1× Ténéré 700 World Raid) · 11–22 October 2026

Rooms per night: 3 riders → book 1 twin/double + 1 single (or 3 singles). Prices below are per double/twin room per night — reckon on roughly 1.4–1.6× that for the whole group per night. All prices are indicative (Oct 2026, not live) — confirm your exact dates when booking.

🅿️ Parking: every hotel here has private/on-site parking. Booking sites don't distinguish motorcycles, so message each hotel to confirm 3 bikes can be parked inside the walls/garage overnight (ready-made message at the bottom, EN + FR).

📅 Book early, in this order: ① Merzouga, Dades, Marrakech (fill up fast) → ② the ferry (cabins sell out) → ③ the rest.

🛳️ Ferry — Almería ↔ Nador (book both crossings)

  • Operators: Baleària · Armas Trasmediterránea
  • Crossing: ~6–7.5 h; night crossings with cabins available → take an outside (sea-view) cabin.
  • How to book: select route + date → add each motorcycle as a vehicle (book all 3 bikes) → add cabin = exterior. Riders can share one or two cabins to save cost.
  • Indicative price: bike supplement from ~€30 each; cabin from ~€180–200; reckon ~€150–260 return pp incl. bike (+ cabin). 2026 fares/timetables were only partly published at research time — confirm when Oct 2026 sailings open.
  • Tips: book early (cabins sell out); arrive 3 h before departure; documents ready (passport, vehicle registration/logbook, green card valid for Morocco, licence). You fill a Moroccan temporary vehicle import (D16ter) form on arrival — keep it for exit.
