🛳️ Ferry — Almería ↔ Nador

  • Operators: Baleària · Armas Trasmediterránea
  • Crossing: ~6–7.5 h; take a night crossing with an outside cabin.
  • Book: add each motorcycle as a vehicle (3 bikes) + exterior cabin; riders can share cabins.
  • Tips: book early; arrive 3 h before departure; documents ready (passport, vehicle registration/logbook, green card valid for Morocco, licence). Fill the Moroccan temporary vehicle import (D16ter) on arrival — keep it for exit.
