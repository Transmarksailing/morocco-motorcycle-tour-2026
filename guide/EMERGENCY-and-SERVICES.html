🛠️ Breakdown & recovery — how it really works

  • There is NO ANWB/AA-style national motorcycle breakdown service in Morocco. Don't expect a phone-a-tow network.
  • On rural roads the Gendarmerie Royale (177) is the de-facto first responder — they secure the scene and can summon a local tow (dépanneuse) or garage, but they don't repair.
  • Recovery is local & informal: the nearest town's garage sends a flatbed/pickup — agree a price up front (negotiate). Small towns fix small bikes; big-bike expertise/parts are limited.
  • Your real safety net is your own insurance. Carry your green card (extend the Morocco cover at the border/ferry) and your insurer's international assistance number — written on paper, not only in your phone. ANWB does not run in-country moto recovery in Morocco; check your policy (ADAC and some international policies do cover Morocco).
  • Be self-sufficient: tools, tubeless plug/repair kit, mini-compressor or CO₂, spare tube(s) (Ténéré), spare chain link, levers, zip-ties, tape. For a serious mechanical failure, plan to truck the bike to Marrakech or Casablanca.

🛞 Tyres — the reality for R1250GS & Ténéré 700

  • Don't assume your size is available on the road. Adventure/knobbly and premium touring sizes for big bikes (GS 19"/17", Ténéré 21"/18") are scarce outside Marrakech and Casablanca.
  • Best stock: Casablanca — BMW Motorrad Maroc 0522 40 07 00 (verify) for the GS; MIFA/Yamaha 0522 04 06 50 and shops (ETS Samouh, Motomania) for the Ténéré. Marrakech = best in the south. Call ahead to confirm your exact size.
  • Advice: carry a proper tubeless repair/plug kit + spare tube(s). If you run an unusual size, bring a spare tyre or pre-arrange one at a Casablanca/Marrakech dealer before departure.

🧰 Documents to carry (each rider)

Passport · vehicle registration/logbook · driving licence (+ IDP) · green card valid for Morocco · insurer assistance number · temporary vehicle import (D16ter, from the border) · travel/health insurance details · blood group noted. Keep paper copies.

Ride within your limits, keep the group together, and carry water. Most "emergencies" on this route are a puncture or a tired bike — a repair kit and 177 cover the rest. Safe travels. 🏍️
