## 🛳️ Ferry — Almería ↔ Nador (book both crossings)

- **Operators:** [Baleària](https://www.balearia.com/en/routes-timetables/ferry-almeria-nador) · [Armas Trasmediterránea](https://armastrasmediterranea.com/en/routes-timetables/ferry-almeria-nador)
- **Crossing:** ~6–7.5 h; **night crossings with cabins** available → take an **outside (sea-view) cabin**.
- **How to book:** select route + date → add **each motorcycle as a vehicle** (book all 3 bikes) → add **cabin = exterior**. Riders can share one or two cabins to save cost.
- **Indicative price:** bike supplement from ~€30 each; cabin from ~€180–200; reckon ~€150–260 return pp incl. bike (+ cabin). *2026 fares/timetables were only partly published at research time — confirm when Oct 2026 sailings open.*
- **Tips:** book early (cabins sell out); **arrive 3 h before departure**; have documents ready (passport, vehicle registration/logbook, green card valid for Morocco, licence). You fill a Moroccan **temporary vehicle import (D16ter)** form on arrival — keep it for exit.
