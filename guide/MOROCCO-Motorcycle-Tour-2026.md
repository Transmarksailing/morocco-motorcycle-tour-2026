## 🗺️ The tour at a glance

**Moraira → Almería (ferry) → Nador → Guercif → Midelt → Ifrane/Azrou → Merzouga (Sahara) → Todra & Dades → Aït Benhaddou → Marrakech → Ouzoud → Beni Mellal → Midelt → Nador (ferry) → Almería → Moraira**

A grand loop from Moraira: a short run down the coast to Almería, the night ferry to Nador, then ten days through the very best of Morocco. From the green Middle Atlas with its cedar forests and Barbary macaques, down the Ziz Valley into the Sahara and the dunes of Erg Chebbi, through the spectacular Todra and Dades gorges, past the UNESCO kasbah of Aït Benhaddou, over the legendary Tizi n'Tichka pass to Marrakech, and back north via the Ouzoud waterfalls to the port.

> **Why October?** The ideal month for Morocco: daytime 20–30 °C, comfortable desert temperatures, crisp mornings in the Atlas, little rain, clear skies and fewer tourists than in summer.

## 🏍️ Our three bikes — group notes

We're riding **two BMW R1250GS** and **one Yamaha Ténéré 700 World Raid** — three bikes, three riders, all adventure/dual-sport machines. The route follows scenic **tarmac** by default, but since all three bikes are off-road capable, the optional gravel sections are open to the whole group (dry weather only; a fully loaded GS is heavier in deep gravel). No stage requires it — every day can also be ridden entirely on tarmac.

- **⛽ Fuel range & group stops.** The Ténéré 700 World Raid (**23 L**, ± 400 km) and the two **R1250GS** (± 20 L, ± 350 km) all have good range. Refuel together at ± 250–280 km, and never skip a station before a desert or mountain stage (days 4, 5 and 10).
- **👥 Riding in formation.** Ride in a staggered formation, with the least experienced rider second so the leader sets the pace and the last rider (sweep) keeps everyone together. Agree hand signals and a fixed regroup point (e.g. every junction/fuel stop).
- **🛣️ Tarmac, gravel & luggage.** All three bikes are off-road capable, so the optional gravel sections (e.g. Cirque de Jaffar) are open to the whole group — dry weather only, and take it easy on a fully loaded GS. Every stage also has a full tarmac line if anyone prefers it. **No deep/soft sand** on laden touring bikes, though — leave the dunes to guides/quads.
- **📄 Documents × 3.** Each bike/rider needs their **own** set of papers (see border checklist). Green card + temporary Moroccan vehicle import per vehicle.
- **🔧 One shared set of tools + spares** is fine, but per bike carry: puncture-repair kit/pump, brake pads, chain lube (World Raid), and a set of fuses. Tyre sizes and oil differ per make.

## 🚢 The crossing — Almería ↔ Nador

The ferry takes ± 6–8 hours across the Mediterranean. Ideally take a **night boat with a cabin** in both directions, so you don't lose a riding day and arrive in Nador fresh in the morning.

- **Booking:** [Baleària](https://www.balearia.com/en) or [Trasmediterranea/Naviera Armas](https://www.navieraarmas.com/en) — choose **Almería → Nador** (not Melilla; Nador is in Morocco itself, while Melilla is Spanish and involves an extra border crossing).
- **Bike + cabin:** book an **outside cabin** and state that you're travelling with motorcycles (lashed down on the vehicle deck).
- **Arrival Nador:** port of **Beni Ansar**. Allow **1–2 hours** for border formalities (passport stamp + temporary vehicle import).
- 🛳️ [Timetable & overview — Directferries](https://www.directferries.com/almeria_nador_ferry.htm)

> 💡 **Tip:** book the return (day 10, Nador → Almería) with some margin; arrive at the terminal at least **3 hours** before departure.

### 🏍️ Day 4 — Thursday 15 October: Midelt → Merzouga (Ziz Valley & the Sahara)
**Route:** Midelt → Errachidia → Ziz Valley (N13) → Erfoud → Merzouga · **Riding time:** ± 4.5 h · **Distance:** 285 km · **Scenery:** ★★★★★

- 🏞️ **Ziz Valley (N13)** · The great transition from mountains to desert: a green ribbon of tens of thousands of date palms between red-brown rock walls. Stop at the **Ziz viewpoint**.
  📍 [Ziz Valley viewpoint](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=31.6870,-4.2570) · 📷 [Photos](https://www.google.com/search?tbm=isch&q=Ziz+Valley+Morocco)

  📍 [Merzouga / Erg Chebbi](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=31.0990,-4.0130) · 📷 [Photos](https://www.google.com/search?tbm=isch&q=Erg+Chebbi+Merzouga+dunes)
- 🏨 **Overnight — Kasbah Mohayut (Hassi Labied)** · A kasbah right by the Erg Chebbi dunes with sunset views; **parking inside the walled kasbah** — secure for the 3 bikes. Alternatives: **Riad Madu** or **Kasbah Erg Chebbi** (both walled, by the dunes).
  🛏️ [Kasbah Mohayut](https://www.hotelmohayut.com/) · 🛏️ [Riad Madu](https://www.riadmadu.com/en/) · 📍 [Merzouga / Erg Chebbi](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=31.0990,-4.0130)

> ⚠️ **Sand!** Don't ride into the dunes on laden touring bikes. Park on firm ground at the lodge; the soft sand is for local guides/quads.

### 🏍️ Day 7 — Sunday 18 October: Aït Benhaddou → Marrakech (Tizi n'Tichka)
**Route:** Aït Benhaddou → Tizi n'Tichka (N9) → Marrakech · **Riding time:** ± 4 h · **Distance:** 195 km · **Highest point:** 2,260 m · **Scenery:** ★★★★★

- 🏔️ **Tizi n'Tichka pass (N9) — 2,260 m** · Classic High Atlas riding: endless bends over the highest tarmac pass of the trip. Recently widened and resurfaced — a joy to ride. Dress warmly; it's chilly at the top.
  📍 [Tizi n'Tichka](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=31.2960,-7.3810) · 📷 [Photos](https://www.google.com/search?tbm=isch&q=Tizi+n+Tichka+pass)
- 🫖 **Optional — Telouet** · The hidden kasbah of the Glaoui pasha, a short detour off the pass.
  📍 [Kasbah Telouet](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=31.2870,-7.2400)
- 🕌 **Arrival Marrakech** · Dive into the red city. **Note: busy traffic** on the medina's edge — ride defensively.
  📍 [Marrakech — Jemaa el-Fna](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=31.6258,-7.9891) · 📷 [Photos](https://www.google.com/search?tbm=isch&q=Marrakech+Jemaa+el+Fna)
- 🏨 **Overnight — Kenzi Rose Garden (Hivernage)** · A hotel just outside the medina with its **own enclosed parking garage** — by far the most reliable option for 3 bikes in Marrakech. Closer to the medina with private **basement motorcycle parking**: **Riad Marrakech By Hivernage**.
  🛏️ [Kenzi Rose Garden](https://www.kenzi-hotels.com/en/kenzi-rose-garden) · 🛏️ [Riad Marrakech By Hivernage](https://www.booking.com/hotel/ma/batoule.html) · 📍 [Marrakech](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=31.6258,-7.9891)
- 🌃 **Evening — Jemaa el-Fna** · The famous square with food stalls, musicians and snake charmers.

> 🅿️ **Bike tip Marrakech:** at Kenzi Rose Garden/Hivernage you simply park safely at the hotel. If you do choose a riad deep in the medina, don't ride into the narrow alleys — use **parking gardienné** (a guarded lot at the medina edge) and continue on foot/by taxi.

## 🛃 Border & documents checklist (per bike × 3)

Make sure **each rider** carries these papers, complete and with copies:

- ✅ **Passport** (still valid ≥ 6 months)
- ✅ **Ferry booking confirmation** (outbound + return)
- ✅ **Vehicle registration document (V5C logbook)** — in your own name, or a signed authorisation letter if not
- ✅ **Driving licence** (UK photocard; an International Driving Permit is recommended)
- ✅ **Insurance certificate / green card** with Morocco cover (check that Morocco is listed on your green card; if not, take out cover at the border on arrival)
- ✅ **GB registration / UK number plate** clearly displayed (a "UK" sticker if your plate doesn't show it)
- ✅ **Temporary vehicle import (D16 / Déclaration)** — filled in on entry; keep it safe, you hand it back on departure
- ✅ Copies of everything (on paper + a photo on your phone)

> ⚠️ **Important:** on entry you receive a temporary import document per vehicle. **Don't lose it** — failing to hand it back on departure causes problems. Don't overstay the permitted period unintentionally.

## 🇲🇦 Mini Morocco handbook for the road

- **Checkpoints:** police/gendarmerie checks are normal. Stop calmly, greet, show your papers, smile. Usually you're waved through.
- **Speed:** in built-up areas often 40–60 km/h; speed cameras occur. Stick to the signs.
- **Fuel:** plenty in the north/cities; before desert and mountain stages (days 4, 5, 10) **always fill up**.
- **Water & food:** drink bottled water; tagine, couscous, brochettes and fresh mint tea are safe and delicious everywhere.
- **Money:** the dirham (MAD) can't be traded freely outside Morocco — change/withdraw locally and keep some cash for small villages.
- **Language:** Arabic + Berber; French is the second language. A few words of French/Arabic open doors.
- **Tips & guides:** a small tip for parking attendants (*gardien*) is customary (5–10 MAD).
- **Emergency numbers:** police **19**, gendarmerie (outside towns) **177**, ambulance **15**.
